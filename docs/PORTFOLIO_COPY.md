## Short LinkedIn project post
I built a **Healthcare Operations & Revenue Analytics** Power BI case study focused on a key question: how do patient access, care operations, and claims/payment outcomes connect?

Across 1,200 encounters, the dataset showed a 74.1% completion rate and 10.0% no-show rate. Revenue analysis identified $77,737 in pending payments, with Cardiology carrying the highest departmental pending balance. I used the dashboard to translate those patterns into targeted recommendations for capacity review, appointment follow-up and revenue-cycle triage.
